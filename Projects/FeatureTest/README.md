# FeatureTest

The engine's sample project: a scene for every feature, each small enough to read. It is the
project the dev tree opens when none is named (`Editor.exe`, `--game-mode`, `--benchmark`,
`Game.exe`, the tests and `Scripts\PackageGame.bat`).

| Scene (`Assets/Scenes/`) | Shows |
|---|---|
| `Default.yaml` | The startup scene: meshes, materials and textures, lights and shadows, and a script (`Scripts/PlayerScript.lua`) turning the room with properties set in the inspector. |
| `Animated.yaml` | Skinned animation: two animated `TwoBoneStrip`s (`Models/Animated`) beside a rigid cube. |
| `StateMachine.yaml` | Animator controllers: a `TwoBoneStrip` whose animator runs `Animations/TwoBoneStrip.animctrl` (Bending and Lifting, switched by the `Lifted` bool over half a second, and the `Wave` trigger starting Bending again from any state); `Scripts/StateMachineDemo.lua` sets `Wave` once after a second, flips `Lifted` every two seconds and logs every state change. |
| `AnimationAdvanced.yaml` | Blend trees, root motion and IK: a `TwoBoneStrip` whose animator runs `Animations/Locomotion.animctrl`, a 1D tree of Bend and Lift that `Scripts/AnimationDemo.lua` sweeps from 0 to 1 and back; an arm (`Models/Animated/ThreeBoneArm.gltf`, generated by `Tools/Animation/make_three_bone_arm.py`) walking by root motion, put back every metre; and an arm whose hand reaches a target moving in a circle through an IK goal. |
| `Hud.yaml` | Game UI: a canvas with a translucent top bar, a logo, a title, a health bar and a button, its text in the engine's Karla font. |
| `Audio.yaml` | Audio: a cube playing the looping `Audio/Tone440.wav` to the right of the camera, which carries the audio listener. |
| `Prefabs.yaml` | Prefabs: two instances of `Prefabs/LampPost.prefab`, the second overriding its lamp's colour and intensity. |
| `Saves.yaml` | Save games: `Scripts/SaveDemo.lua` turns its cube, saves on frame 30 and loads that save on frame 60, logging both; the cube jumps back to where it was saved. |
| `Physics.yaml` | Physics: a stack of three boxes, a bouncing ball, a capsule that topples, a kinematic paddle (`Scripts/Paddle.lua`) swinging into a crate, and a trigger zone on the `Trigger` layer (named in `engine_settings.yaml`). `Scripts/PhysicsDemo.lua`, on the ball, pushes it as Play starts, logs the zone it falls through and the first thing it lands on, and casts a ray down onto the stack on frame 10. Turn on the toolbar's Colliders toggle to see every collider. |
| *(textures)* | `Textures/Luts/Neutral.png` and `Warm.png` are colour grading LUTs (1024 x 32) for a `PostProcessComponent`'s `ColorLut`, written by `Tools/PostProcess/make_color_luts.py`; paint over `Neutral.png` to make your own. |
| `Pbr.yaml` | Physically based rendering: two rows of spheres, dielectric red and metal gold, from roughness 0 (left) to 1 (right) (`Materials/Pbr`), the DamagedHelmet with its imported material (`Models/DamagedHelmet/DamagedHelmet_Material_MR.material`), a sun casting shadows on the floor, and an `EnvironmentComponent` lighting everything with `Environments/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky_1k.hdr` (Poly Haven, CC0) and showing it as the sky. |
| `Transparency.yaml` | Alpha modes: three glass spheres at alpha 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 overlapping each other in front of an opaque block (Transparent, blended back to front), a tinted double-sided glass pane, and a wooden fence (`Textures/Fence.png`, generated: slats and rails whose gaps have alpha 0) that is a double-sided Cutoff material, so its gaps are holes in it and in its shadow from the sun; the Poly Haven sky (`Materials/Transparency`, `Models/Plane/plane.obj`). |
| `Character.yaml` | A character controller: the player (`Scripts/CharacterDemo.lua`) walks along +x through a trigger zone, up a 25 degree ramp, over a platform and down two 0.2 m steps into a crate it pushes (`Scripts/CharacterCrate.lua` logs it) towards a 1 m wall, jumping at 3 s. Turn on the toolbar's Colliders toggle to see its capsule in magenta. |
| `Joints.yaml` | Joints: a door on a hinge (pushed open by `Scripts/JointsDemo.lua`, which also finds the chain with an overlap and the door with a sphere cast), a chain of three boxes on distance joints, a platform on a slider and a ball on a spring. Turn on the toolbar's Colliders toggle to see the joints in green. |
| `Terrain.yaml` | Terrain: a 128 m tile of hills rising to a snowy ridge (`Terrain/Hills.terrain`, generated), its four layers (`Textures/Terrain`, generated: grass, a dirt path, rock on the slopes and snow) blended by its splat map, under the Poly Haven sky; two boxes and a ball drop onto its height-field collider. Select `Hills` to sculpt or paint it in the Scene view. |
| `ParticlesBenchmark.yaml` | A cloud of 100,000 alpha-blended particles (`Particles/Benchmark.particle`) over the ground, for `Editor.exe --benchmark 600 ParticlesBenchmark.yaml`. |
| `Particles.yaml` | Particles: a campfire of additive flames (`Particles/Fire.particle`) under rising alpha smoke (`Particles/Smoke.particle`, a 4 x 4 flipbook), and sparks bursting off an anvil every 1.5 s and falling (`Particles/Sparks.particle`), their textures generated (`Textures/Particles`), lit by a warm point light under the Poly Haven night sky. They play only in Play mode. |
| `GameFlowMenu.yaml` | Game flow: a menu (`Scripts/GameFlowMenu.lua`) whose PLAY button loads `GameFlowLevel.yaml` with `Scene.load`, OPTIONS opens `Options.yaml` and QUIT calls `Application.quit()`. |
| `Loading.yaml` | A loading screen: `Scripts/LoadingScreen.lua` loads `Pbr.yaml` in the background with `Scene.loadAsync`, filling the bar from `Scene.loadProgress()` while this scene keeps rendering, and `Pbr.yaml` takes over once it is loaded. Try `Editor.exe --game-mode 600 Loading.yaml`. |
| `Options.yaml` | Player settings: an options menu (`Scripts/OptionsMenu.lua`). Each value button cycles its setting (resolution, window mode, vsync, frame cap, master and music volume, quality) or, for the Submit key, waits for the next key pressed; APPLY uses the changes, SAVE keeps them and the key in the player's folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%/HedgehogEngine/FeatureTest/Player`), REVERT goes back to the saved ones, Esc returns to the menu. In the editor only the volumes apply; the rest applies in `Game.exe`. |
| `GameFlowLevel.yaml` | Game flow: a level whose script (`Scripts/GameFlowLevel.lua`) locks the cursor and turns the camera with the mouse, counts visits in `GameState` and returns to the menu on `UiCancel` (Escape). |
| `benchmark.yaml` | The renderer benchmark: a 5×5 grid of DamagedHelmets with their imported material (`Editor.exe --benchmark`, see `PERFORMANCE.md`). |
| `test.yaml` | A small mesh-and-material test scene. |

Every scene here is played by ScriptingTest's `test_feature_test_scenes.cpp`: it loads, references only files that\r
exist, plays 120 frames without an error or warning,
and Stop restores it. A scene added to `Assets/Scenes` is covered with no change to the test.

Try the game flow with `Editor.exe --game-mode 600 GameFlowMenu.yaml` (PLAY with the mouse, or an arrow key and then Enter; Esc back, QUIT ends the run).
Try one with `Binaries\windows-x86_64\Debug\Editor.exe --game-mode 600 Audio.yaml`, or open it in
the editor from the Project panel. Third-party content here is listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
at the repository root.
